What FundSeek is

FundSeek helps disabled adults, families, caregivers and veterans browse funding programs and resources. The eight fund types in the app today are: Additional resources, Grants, Loans, Initiatives, Scholarships, Discounts, Tax credits and Waivers. Funding freedoms. Finding abilities.

FundSeek is not affiliated with the VA or any government agency.

How to use it today

  1. Open the app and tap Get started.
  2. On consent, choose I agree or Not now, just browse.
  3. If you agree, you can note your state, what you need, whether you are a veteran or service member, and what you would like help funding (Housing, Equipment, Transportation, Education, and more), or tap Skip on any step. No account needed.
  4. Open a fund-type tile (Additional resources, Grants, Loans, Initiatives, Scholarships, Discounts, Tax credits or Waivers) to browse programs and see who each program helps, what it covers and its official link. Official program links open in your web browser.

Stays on this phone. Never shared. Your answers save only on this phone. You can edit them on Profile or wipe them with Delete my info on the About screen. Nothing is sent to FundSeek.

FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. You can use FundSeek without an account.

Where do my answers go?

They are saved only on this phone in secure storage. You can edit them on Profile. Delete my info wipes them. Nothing is sent to FundSeek.

How do I delete my info?

Open About and tap Delete my info. That wipes everything saved for FundSeek on this phone and cancels local reminders.

Is my info sent anywhere?

No. It stays on this phone and is never sent to FundSeek.

Does my state change results?

Yes. If you pick a state, FundSeek shows programs for that state plus nationwide ones. If you choose Prefer not to say, FundSeek shows nationwide ones.

Can I open a site from the app?

If you open a link to an outside site, it opens in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies. FundSeek does not send them your answers.

Who is this for?

Disabled adults, families, caregivers and veterans. The app never assumes the reader has a disability.

Accessibility

We aim for clear labels, readable type, and tap areas of at least 44 points on primary controls.

Text follows your iPhone's text size.

Referral codes and affiliate links

Some Discounts links or codes may earn FundSeek a commission. It never changes your price or what we show you. The link or code is the same for everyone and never includes your answers. The store's site has its own privacy rules.
